FINANCIAL RESULTS

1. Revenue
Consolidated revenue for Q2 FY2027 was US$7,642 million.
Revenue for Q2 FY2026 was US$7,466 million.
Year-over-year revenue growth was 2.4% in US dollars.
Quarter-over-quarter revenue growth was 0.2% in US dollars.

2. Operating Margin
The operating margin for Q2 FY2027 was 24.0%.

3. Net Profit
Net profit for Q2 FY2027 was US$1,450 million.
Net profit for Q2 FY2026 was US$1,464 million.

4. Annualized AI Revenue
Annualized AI revenue reached US$3.1 billion in Q2 FY2027.
The company stated that this exceeded 10% of revenue.

5. Total Contract Value
Total Contract Value of deals signed during Q2 FY2027 was US$9.6 billion.

6. Net Cash from Operations
Net cash from operations was US$1.48 billion.
This represented 102.2% of net income, as reported by TCS.

7. Dividend
TCS declared a second interim dividend of ₹12 per equity share.
The company announced October 14, 2026, as the record date and October 30, 2026, as the payment date.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION RULES

- These results are for Tata Consultancy Services Limited, not Tata Group as a whole.
- Distinguish revenue from net profit.
- Distinguish operating margin from net margin.
- Revenue growth in US dollars and constant currency growth are different measures.
