FINANCIAL RESULTS

1. Revenue from Operations

Consolidated revenue from operations for FY2025-26 was ₹146,885 crore.
Consolidated revenue from operations for FY2024-25 was ₹128,218 crore.
Year-over-year growth was approximately 14.6%.

2. EBITDA

Consolidated EBITDA for FY2025-26 was ₹76,255 crore.
Consolidated EBITDA for FY2024-25 was ₹64,170 crore.
Year-over-year growth was approximately 18.8%.

3. EBITDA Margin

The consolidated EBITDA margin for FY2025-26 was 51.9%.
The consolidated EBITDA margin for FY2024-25 was approximately 50.0%.

4. Profit After Tax

Consolidated profit after tax for FY2025-26 was ₹30,049 crore.
Consolidated profit after tax for FY2024-25 was ₹26,109 crore.
Year-over-year growth was approximately 15.1%.

5. Customer Base

Reliance Jio Infocomm Limited served 524.4 million customers in India as of March 31, 2026.

This customer-base figure refers to the licensed operating entity, Reliance Jio Infocomm Limited (RJIL).
