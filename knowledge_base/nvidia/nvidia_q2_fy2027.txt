FINANCIAL RESULTS

1. Total Revenue
NVIDIA reported revenue of $96.221 billion in Q2 FY2027.
Revenue in Q2 FY2026 was $46.743 billion.
Year-over-year revenue growth was 106%.
Quarter-over-quarter revenue growth was 18%.

2. Data Center Revenue
Data Center revenue was approximately $89.0 billion in Q2 FY2027.
It increased by 117% compared with the same quarter of the previous fiscal year.

3. GAAP Gross Margin
GAAP gross margin was 75.0% in Q2 FY2027.
GAAP gross margin was 72.4% in Q2 FY2026.

4. GAAP Net Income
GAAP net income was $59.688 billion in Q2 FY2027.
GAAP net income was $26.422 billion in Q2 FY2026.

5. GAAP Diluted Earnings Per Share
GAAP diluted earnings per share were $2.46 in Q2 FY2027.
GAAP diluted earnings per share were $1.08 in Q2 FY2026.

6. Revenue Outlook
NVIDIA expected revenue of $108.0 billion, plus or minus 2%, for Q3 FY2027.
This is management's outlook, not a confirmed actual result.
