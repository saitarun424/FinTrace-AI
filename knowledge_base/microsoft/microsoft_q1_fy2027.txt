FINANCIAL RESULTS

1. Total Revenue

Microsoft reported total revenue of $92.279 billion in Q1 FY2027.
Revenue in Q1 FY2026 was $77.673 billion.
Year-over-year revenue growth was 19%.

2. Operating Income

Operating income was $45.915 billion in Q1 FY2027.
Operating income in Q1 FY2026 was $37.961 billion.
Year-over-year operating income growth was 21%.

3. Net Income

GAAP net income was $36.629 billion in Q1 FY2027.
GAAP net income in Q1 FY2026 was $27.747 billion.
Year-over-year GAAP net income growth was 32%.

Non-GAAP net income was $36.422 billion in Q1 FY2027.
Non-GAAP net income in Q1 FY2026 was $30.833 billion.
Year-over-year non-GAAP net income growth was 18%.

4. Diluted Earnings Per Share

GAAP diluted earnings per share were $4.92 in Q1 FY2027.
GAAP diluted earnings per share were $3.72 in Q1 FY2026.

Non-GAAP diluted earnings per share were $4.89 in Q1 FY2027.
Non-GAAP diluted earnings per share were $4.13 in Q1 FY2026.

5. Microsoft Cloud Revenue

Microsoft Cloud revenue was $63.9 billion in Q1 FY2027.
It increased by 30% year over year.
The constant-currency growth rate was 31%.

Microsoft Cloud revenue is a business metric and should not be confused with total company revenue.

6. Azure Revenue

Azure revenue was $33.165 billion in Q1 FY2027.
Azure revenue was $22.384 billion in Q1 FY2026.
Azure revenue increased by 48% year over year.

7. Other Business Highlights

Revenue in Agents and Infra was $76.795 billion.
Revenue in Devices and Consumer was $15.484 billion.
Microsoft returned $10.2 billion to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases during the quarter.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION RULES

* GAAP and non-GAAP figures are different measures. Never mix them.
* Non-GAAP figures adjust for the impact of investments in OpenAI.
* Revenue and net income are different financial metrics.
* Microsoft Cloud revenue is different from total company revenue.
* Azure revenue is a separate product and service offering metric.
* Always specify the fiscal quarter and currency.
* Treat year-over-year growth as a comparison with the corresponding quarter of the previous fiscal year.
* Do not invent figures when the source does not provide them.
* Financial statement figures are reported in millions of USD in the original release; the values above are converted to billions where appropriate.
* Distinguish reported results from management forecasts.
